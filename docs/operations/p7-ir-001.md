# P7-IR-001 Incident Response, On-call and Cost Controls

## Scope and source of truth

This runbook applies to CityChatbot S0–S3 incidents. The incident record,
severity, owner, status, evidence digest and kill-switch state are the source
of truth; an LLM, dashboard narrative or status message cannot change them.
Every action is tenant-scoped, idempotent and append-only audited.

Severity follows `fullspec.md`:

- **S0** — tenant isolation breach, secret/PII leak or authentication bypass.
- **S1** — critical wrong fact affecting rights, safety, cost, time or law;
  core outage; material queue/cost failure.
- **S2** — unsupported noncritical fact, wrong department/source or degraded
  noncritical function.
- **S3** — copy, format or completeness defect without a factual error.

Any S0/S1 occurrence triggers the affected auto-answer or outbound feature's
kill switch, evidence preservation, last-known-good rollback and recertification
before reopening. The narrowest safe scope is chosen in this order:

`FEATURE → MODEL/PROMPT/INDEX → TENANT → GLOBAL`.

## Roles, escalation and communication

The incident commander owns the timeline and decision log. SRE owns service
containment/recovery, SECURITY owns isolation and credential rotation, the AI
owner owns model/prompt/index rollback, Messaging owns LINE/provider handling,
BE owns queue/data reconciliation, and PO/Comms owns customer-facing wording.

The commander must publish an internal status update at declaration and after
each material containment/recovery decision. Tenant/public updates contain only
impact, current safe behavior, next update time and support path. They must not
contain prompts, document text, provider payloads, tokens, PII or unverified
root-cause claims. Use incident ID and redacted evidence digest for correlation.

## Failure matrix

| Category | Detect | Contain | Recover/reconcile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tenant isolation breach | RLS denial anomaly or cross-tenant sentinel | Disable affected tenant, revoke session, freeze cache/vector path | Verify RLS/composite scope, reconcile records, rotate credentials, recertify |
| Wrong answer | Claim/citation verifier, negative feedback or S1 review | Force `HANDOFF`, freeze affected model/prompt/index | Correct approved source/policy, run locked suite, publish versioned fix |
| Secret leak | Secret scan, provider alert or access-log anomaly | Revoke/rotate credential and disable smallest integration | Remove secret from artifacts/logs/cache, verify blast radius, recertify |
| LINE/provider outage | 429/5xx/quota/webhook lag/delivery alert | Pause noncritical broadcast; keep complaint/outbox; bounded retry/DLQ | Verify provider, replay idempotent jobs, reconcile accepted messages |
| Queue backlog | Queue depth/age, lease timeout or DLQ alert | Pause amplifying consumer; protect tenant fairness and complaint intake | Fix worker/dependency, reconcile expected jobs, replay selected DLQ |
| Cost spike | AI/LINE/storage/egress/seat/workload budget | Apply 70/90/100 policy and safe handoff | Identify driver, correct budget/config, restore only with owner approval |

## Cost and quota controls

Budget arithmetic is deterministic and uses trusted usage/limit values:

- **70%**: warn the budget owner and record an alert.
- **90%**: restrict noncritical AI and alert SRE/AI/PO; core complaint and
  manual workflows remain available.
- **100%**: force safe `HANDOFF` for noncritical AI and pause optional spend;
  core complaint intake must not stop.

The controller covers LINE API, AI tokens, storage/egress, active documents,
staff seats and complaints/tickets. Never infer cost from an AI-generated
summary. Restore a restricted feature only after usage arithmetic, owner,
tenant scope and rollback target are recorded.

## Evidence preservation and postmortem

Preserve request/correlation IDs, timestamps, redacted logs, deployment/config
digest, job IDs and SHA-256 evidence digests. Do not copy raw prompts, source
documents, provider bodies, credentials or PII into the incident record.

The postmortem must contain: incident ID, severity/category, timeline, impact,
root cause, detection, containment, recovery, customer communication, evidence
digests, follow-up Task IDs, owner and reviewer. Each corrective action has an
owner and due date; unresolved risk remains an explicitly tracked Task.

## Tabletop/game-day checklist

Run at least these six synthetic cases without production PII or broadcast:

1. tenant isolation breach (S0);
2. wrong grounded answer (S1);
3. secret leak/credential rotation (S0);
4. LINE provider outage (S1);
5. queue backlog/DLQ growth (S1);
6. AI cost spike at 70% → 90% → 100%.

For each case, record detect/contain/recover timestamps, commander, kill-switch
scope, evidence digest, status update and reconciliation result. A failed
exercise opens a follow-up Task; it does not get hidden by a green dashboard.

## Rollback

1. Pause the affected consumer/provider integration and retain queued/DLQ data.
2. Activate the narrowest approved kill switch and force safe handoff where
   answer correctness or cost safety is uncertain.
3. Restore the previous signed artifact/config/model/index/theme and preserve
   the incident evidence chain.
4. Reconcile complaints, tickets, notifications, jobs, audit and cost usage.
5. Re-run targeted unit/static/build/synthetic checks and recertify before
   releasing the switch.
